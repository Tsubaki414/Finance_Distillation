# 2026-10-05 (eve) — PM relaxation pass on COMPOSE

The morning root-cause fix (`docs/2026-10-05_root_cause_compose.md`) was right about the
cause (fat packs + data-led openings) but over-corrected the budget. Judgment posts were
starved: one supporting fact and a 2-number cap left no room for "call + levels". This pass
loosens the number budget and two noisy soft checks. Everything is still soft.

## What was too strict → what it is now

| Knob | Before | Now | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| `RECIPES['judgment_take']` / `['contrarian_take']` supporting facts | 1 | **2** (= `JUDGMENT_MAX_FACTS`, trim still caps at 2) | `live/compose.py` |
| `EVIDENCE_BUDGET['max_numbers']` | 2 | **3** | `live/compose.py` |
| `INFO_DUMP_NUMBERS` (info_dump fires at ≥N distinct numbers) | 4 | **5** (≥3 data-clause rule unchanged) | `live/compose.py` |
| info_dump repair note / `qa_levels.FIXES['info_dump']` | "at most 2 numbers" | "at most 3 numbers" | `live/compose.py`, `live/qa_levels.py` |
| COMPOSE prompt | "at most 2 numbers", "only the one or two numbers" | "at most 3 numbers", "only the few numbers (at most 3)" | `live/compose.py` |
| `zh_industry` / `en_industry` `hard_constraints` | 最多引用 2 个数字 / at most 2 numbers; "用不上的单位就不用" / "Leave unused units unused" | 最多引用 3 个数字 / at most 3 numbers; "多余的单位优先留着不用" / "Prefer leaving surplus units unused" | signature cards |
| `anti_repeat` stylistic_repeat / duplicate_topic | ran with any history (1 prior draft enough) | skipped when the persona has **< 3** prior bodies (`anti_repeat.MIN_HISTORY`) | `live/anti_repeat.py` |

The short-history skip stops first-day personas and fixture re-smokes from matching their own
first draft and triggering a false regenerate. `phrase_ban`, `judgment_label` and
`verify_source` always run.

## Emotion: dry-source clamp (the extra gap picked, Option 1)

`build_emotion_brief` now also returns `source_dry` and `accept_intensity`.

- Dry source = the dominant register has ≤1 emotion-marker hit (`DRY_SOURCE_MAX_MARKERS`)
  and the source has no `!`.
- Clamp: MID/HIGH only, `accept_intensity = min(target, max(target - 1, floor))`, with
  floor 2 for MID and 3 for HIGH (`DRY_CLAMP_FLOOR`). LOW and non-dry sources:
  `accept_intensity == target`.
- `target_intensity` is **not** changed, and the two clamp keys are removed from the payload
  sent to the model, so the prompt still aims at the full target.
- HIGH retry acceptance (`emotion_contract.retry_improved`): keep the retry if it clears an
  emotion finding (as before), **or** if `emotion_drop` still fires but the retry reaches
  `accept_intensity`, is strictly more intense than the first draft, and adds no
  `emotion_overfire`. `emotion_retry.accept_reason` records which rule applied.
- MID still has no emotion retry, by policy. A warn-only record gets `dry_source_ok: true` when
  the draft already reaches the clamp, so reviewers can treat it as low priority.
- The HIGH ceiling and overfire limits are untouched, and the clamp can only lower the bar
  for keeping a retry.

- The ≥3 data-clause info_dump rule and the research_summary checks.
- Soft-only: nothing in this pass is a hard block.
